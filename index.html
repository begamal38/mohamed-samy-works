About Mohamed Samy

Mohamed Samy is a civil engineer and interior designer based in Egypt, blending structural precision with artistic vision to craft spaces that balance durability, comfort, and timeless visual impact.

Backed by hands-on civil engineering site execution, his studio brings more than a decade of finishing and décor experience (as featured in Al Gomhoria Al Youm) to residential apartments, duplexes, villas, and commercial hospitality projects across Beni Suef, Sheikh Zayed, and Cairo.

Standout completed works include After 8 Cafe, BUNO Cafe, a 60 m² raw-to-turnkey apartment in Mokattam delivered in 45 days, Pyramids Heights Compound – Sheikh Zayed, Zahraa El-Maadi – Al Yasmeen, Makkah Compound, and El Nile Corniche – Beni Suef.

Every project is managed end-to-end — from raw brick inspection, MEP rough-ins, and plastering through lighting calibration, stone and timber joinery, and millimetre-checked handover.

How We Work · Raw Brick to Handover
STEP 01 · SITE INTAKE
Site Inspection & Brief

Infrastructure & Site Supervision
