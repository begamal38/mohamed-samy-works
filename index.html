05 / The Architect

About Mohamed Samy

Mohamed Samy is a civil engineer and interior designer based in Egypt, blending structural precision with artistic vision to create spaces that inspire both comfort and sophistication.

With a solid foundation in civil engineering and years of hands-on experience, he has designed and executed a diverse range of residential and commercial projects across Egypt — including Beni Suef, Sheikh Zayed, and Cairo.

His portfolio features standout works such as BUNO Café, Zohour El Maadi, El Nile Corniche – Beni Suef, and Pyramids Heights Compound – Sheikh Zayed, reflecting a consistent commitment to detail, elegance, and timeless design.

Every project is crafted with a balance between function and artistry — turning ordinary spaces into refined architectural statements that speak to the client’s vision and lifestyle.
